Front Page · Firewood & Hearth

The quiet art of
burning well.

Every winter, the same question returns to the kitchens and stoops of the Highveld: what, exactly, is worth burning? At On-Point Wood we hold a simple conviction — that premium thorn firewood, cured for eighteen months under an unforgiving sun, deserves to be treated as an article of care rather than a commodity. Each unit is split, dried and delivered with the same devotion the name suggests. We deliver across Midrand and greater Johannesburg, and we take orders of ten units or more — never fewer, for the fire deserves commitment.

Section III

Questions, answered.

Why a minimum of ten units?+

Firewood logistics are a matter of scale. Ten units keeps delivery viable — and your price per unit honest.

How do I pay?+

We confirm your order on WhatsApp; you then pay on delivery by cash or card, or by EFT using the details we send in the chat.

Will you stack the wood?+

Gladly. Mention it in your order notes and our delivery team will stack it neatly — some say reverently.

Is thorn wood truly better?+

Hotter, longer, cleaner. Soft pine is for people who have not yet been introduced to thorn.
